⚔️ 3.5 TKI Conflict Modes
Two axes: Assertiveness (your concerns) × Cooperativeness (others' concerns)
🏆 Competing
Win-Lose
Use for: emergencies, unpopular decisions. Risk: creates "yes people"
🤝 Collaborating
Win-Win
Use for: both parties matter, learning, commitment. Risk: time-consuming
⚖️ Compromising
Partial Win
Use for: time pressure, equal power, temporary settlement. Risk: loses sight of bigger issues
🚶 Avoiding
Lose-Lose
Use for: trivial issues, cool down. Risk: decisions by default
🎁 Accommodating
Lose-Win
Use for: you're wrong, building goodwill. Risk: ideas ignored
💡 Library book example: Collaborating finds the win-win — ebook, photocopy, reserve a copy — rather than forcing a choice.

📋 3.7 Commitment & Accountability
The Commitment Request Formula
"In order to [context]...
I request you [specific action]
by [date & time]...
Do you commit to this?"
Valid Responses
ResponseStatus
"Yes, I commit"✅ Commitment
"No, I don't commit"❌ Decline
"I'll respond by [time]"⏳ Pending
"I'll do my best"❌ This is NO
"Maybe"❌ This is NO
The Accountability Conversation (7 Steps)
  1. Check your intention — manage emotions first; clarify what you want to achieve
  2. Set the scene
  3. Assert prior commitment — confirm what was agreed
  4. Assert the breakdown — state what didn't happen
  5. Inquire their perspective — listen; distinguish reasonable vs acceptable reasons
  6. Issue damage report — work impact, feelings, relationship impact
  7. Make a new request — another commitment conversation
Acceptable reason: Genuinely outside their control (system down, family emergency)
Not acceptable: "Something came up" — they could have renegotiated
🎯 3.8 Balanced Scorecard
Kaplan & Norton — Four Perspectives
🎯 VISION — What are we trying to achieve?
💰 Financial
How should we appear to stakeholders?
Revenue, costs, profitability, ROI
👥 Customer
How should we appear to customers?
Satisfaction, retention, market share
⚙️ Internal Process
What processes must we excel at?
Quality, efficiency, innovation
🌱 Learning & Growth
How will we sustain ability to change?
Skills, culture, technology
Lead vs Lag: One perspective is the lead indicator (drives the others). Results must be visible — public scoreboards reinforce accountability.
