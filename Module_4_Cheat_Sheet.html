🖼️ 4.1 Problem Framing
Core Principle
  • How we frame a problem shapes how we solve it
  • Frame at the right level of abstraction — not as a solution
  • ❌ "I need to buy a car" → ✅ "I need transport options"
  • Involve others with different perspectives

🔭 4.2 Strategic Thinking Skills
Hughes & Colarelli Beatty — 5 Skills
  • Scanning — analyse the environment, look outward
  • Visioning — communicate a compelling future
  • Reframing — challenge assumptions, see opportunities
  • Making meaning — translate vision into daily decisions
  • Thinking systemically — find high-leverage interventions
Scanning Tools
  • SWOT: Strengths · Weaknesses · Opportunities · Threats
  • STEEPLED: Social · Tech · Economic · Environmental · Political · Legal · Ethical · Demographic
  • Narrows to 3–5 key strategic drivers that shape everything

⚖️ 4.4 Rational Decision Making
6-Step Model (Bazerman & Moore)
  1. Define the problem
  2. Identify the criteria
  3. Assess the criteria
  4. Generate alternatives
  5. Rate each alternative on each criterion
  6. Compute the optimal decision
3 Pre-Decision Steps (Confer)
  • Challenge constraints — question what's actually fixed (Stanford $5 example)
  • Premortem — how could this fail? (Cobra Effect)
  • Check the basics — details matter ($125M NASA Orbiter)

Stage Key Points
  • Empathise: observe, engage, watch, listen — set aside assumptions
  • Define: frame from the user's perspective, not the org's
  • Ideate: defer judgement — diverge first, converge later
  • Prototype: build cheaply, don't get attached, know your question
  • Test: show don't tell, ask users to compare, loop back

📄 Research — Jang (2017)
Cultural Brokerage in Multicultural Teams
Brokerage:Facilitating interactions across cultural boundaries
Insider:Multicultural with overlapping cultures
Outsider:Multicultural with no overlapping cultures
Two Types of Brokerage
  • Integrating (insiders) — synthesise knowledge across cultures
  • Eliciting (outsiders) — draw out cultural knowledge from others
  • Both significantly enhanced team creative performance
Key Findings
  • Multiculturals (both types) boost creativity in diverse teams
  • Together, integrating + eliciting explained 28% of variance in creative performance
  • Monoculturals who attempt to elicit decrease team performance
  • Cultural outsiders are as valuable as insiders — don't overlook them
Leader tip: Identify your cultural brokers. Create conditions for insiders AND outsiders to play that role. Appoint formally if needed.
